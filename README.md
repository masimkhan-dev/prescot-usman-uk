Bilkul. Main isko proposal-style scattered requirements ke bajaye ek clean, premium, client-ready website scope mein arrange kar raha hoon. Is version mein business ki buy/sell model, repair services, Walk-In + Door-to-Door + Mail-In, wheelchair accessibility, WhatsApp lead generation, SEO, aur Phase 2 dashboard sab properly integrated hain.

PHASE 1 — WEBSITE SCOPE & FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION use the logo and start develope the website for uk base client and this wiull be used by uk customers

Prescot Mobiles & Computer Services

Business Type: Mobile Phone, Computer & Gaming Repair + Buy & Sell
Location: 57 Eccleston Street, Prescot, L34 5QH, United Kingdom

01 — WEBSITE OBJECTIVE

The primary objective is to create a modern, professional and conversion-focused website for Prescot Mobiles & Computer Services.

The website will be designed to:

Establish a strong professional online presence

Help local customers discover the business through Google

Showcase repair services clearly

Display phones, laptops, computers, gaming consoles and accessories for sale

Generate enquiries through WhatsApp, phone and online forms

Promote flexible repair options

Build customer trust through reviews and business information

Make it easy for customers to find and visit the physical shop

Create a strong digital foundation for future business growth

The website will be developed with future scalability in mind so that Phase 2 can introduce a dedicated Business Management & Inventory Dashboard.
